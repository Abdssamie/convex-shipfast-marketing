# Organizations

Organizations make FluxKit suitable for B2B and team-based SaaS products. The product includes organization logic, account switching, invitation workflows, and organization settings screens.

## What this feature includes

- Multi-tenant organization data model.
- Organization creation and switching UI.
- Invite and membership workflows.
- Organization settings management.
- Billing alignment at the organization level.

## How to use it

<Steps>
### Decide the tenancy model

Choose whether every user gets a personal workspace, a team workspace, or both.

### Configure organization creation rules

Set naming, slug, and membership defaults that match your product.

### Restrict access by role

Apply role checks to billing, settings, and administrative actions.

### Connect invites to onboarding

Ensure invitation emails, acceptance screens, and post-accept redirects all land users inside the correct organization context.
</Steps>

## Recommended defaults

- Make the current organization explicit in the UI header.
- Limit destructive organization actions to owners.
- Treat invitation emails and revoked memberships as audited events.
