# Authentication

FluxKit ships with modern authentication flows intended for production SaaS products. The product includes sign in, sign up, password reset, email verification, and invite acceptance screens.

## What this feature includes

- Dedicated auth route group for public identity flows.
- Better Auth client and server helpers.
- Verification and recovery screens.
- Invite-based onboarding support.
- Organization-aware account creation paths.

## How to use it

<Steps>
### Configure credentials and secrets

Set the Better Auth secret and the site URLs used by your auth callbacks and emails.

### Wire provider settings

Enable the email and social providers you plan to expose in the sign-in and sign-up flows.

### Customize the route group

Update the auth routes and supporting UI to match your brand, copy, and policy requirements.

### Test every recovery path

Validate sign up, sign in, email verification, magic-link or password reset flows, and invite acceptance before launch.
</Steps>

## Production guidance

- Keep auth callback URLs consistent across preview and production environments.
- Treat email templates as part of the auth surface, not a later add-on.
- Add monitoring for failed sign-in spikes and verification delivery failures.

<Callout type="info">
  Authentication is the feature that most directly affects conversion and
  support load. Treat copy, redirect rules, and verification timing as product
  decisions, not just implementation details.
</Callout>
