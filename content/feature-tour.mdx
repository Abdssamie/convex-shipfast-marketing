# Feature Tour

This page reflects the feature story presented in this marketing site.

## Core value buckets

### Auth and security

- Email/password and magic-link flows.
- Google OAuth sign-in path.
- Password reset and email verification narrative.
- Rate-limiting patterns positioned for safer auth surfaces.

### B2B foundations

- Organization workspaces.
- Team invitations and role controls.
- Billing and account settings surfaces.
- Polar checkout and portal wiring story.

### Product modules

- Dashboard and analytics surfaces.
- Tasks, calendar, mail, chat, and user-directory modules.
- Realtime notifications and reusable UI building blocks.

### Developer experience

- Next.js 16, React 19, and TypeScript stack.
- Convex backend positioning.
- Better Auth and Polar integration points.
- Brevo/Resend email path and monitoring integrations in positioning.

## Buyer interpretation guide

- This marketing project is optimized to communicate value and reduce objections.
- Product modules shown in the marketing story are the scope buyers evaluate before purchase.
- Your custom implementation depth depends on your product-specific requirements after purchase.
