# Overview

FluxKit is positioned as a production-ready SaaS starter with a polished marketing surface and dashboard foundation.

This docs section is intentionally based on what is described and presented in this marketing project.

## What buyers see in this project

- A conversion-focused landing page with hero, feature sections, pricing, and objection-handling FAQ.
- Positioning around skipping boilerplate and reclaiming build time.
- Feature narratives for auth/security, B2B foundations, and product modules.
- A clear pricing path for Pro and Teams licensing.

## Why this docs section exists

- Help buyers understand the current offer before purchase.
- Make feature claims clear and easy to verify from the page content.
- Reduce ambiguity around what is included versus what you customize.

## Buyer checklist

- Review value proposition and capabilities in [Feature Tour](/docs/feature-tour).
- Compare time and cost framing in [Build vs Buy](/docs/build-vs-buy).
- Confirm purchase path at [Pricing](/#pricing).
- Review legal terms at [License](/license).
