# FAQ

## Is FluxKit just a UI kit?

No. This project includes a complete marketing and docs experience designed to help you sell and onboard a SaaS offer faster.

## What is production-ready vs setup-required?

Production-ready in this repo:

- Landing sections, pricing narratives, FAQ handling, and docs IA.
- Docs routed through Nextra at `/docs` with structured navigation.
- Legal and support pages linked from the public surface.

Setup-required before go-live:

- Final checkout URLs and provider-side offer setup.
- Brand/legal review for your business context.
- Ongoing conversion testing and offer iteration.

## Can I use my own auth or billing provider later?

Yes. This marketing project mainly depends on the URLs and labels you expose in pricing and docs.

## Does Teams plan change technical architecture?

In this repository, Teams primarily changes offer framing, license scope, and buyer expectations.

## How long does onboarding take after purchase?

Most teams can deploy quickly, then iterate copy and pricing as they validate conversions. Use [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started).

## Do I need Convex experience to use FluxKit?

Not for this marketing/docs project. Basic Next.js and content editing experience is enough.
