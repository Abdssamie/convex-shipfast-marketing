# Licensing

FluxKit is sold as a commercial starter with plan-specific usage rights.

Use this page as a practical buying guide, not legal advice.

## Plan-level framing

- **Pro:** single-seat commercial usage with lifetime updates.
- **Teams:** Pro rights plus internal multi-seat access and broader team usage.

Always verify current pricing and plan text at [/#pricing](/#pricing).

## What buyers should confirm before purchase

- Who will actively work in the codebase (seat scope).
- Whether one product or multiple internal products are in scope.
- Whether support expectations match the selected tier.

## Technical alignment with licensing

- Pricing and CTA surfaces communicate Pro vs Teams decisions.
- License terms page is the source of truth for usage rights.
- Plan labels in UI are for buyer guidance, not legal override.

## Disclosure

- License rights are legal terms; UI labels are operational hints.
- If there is a conflict, legal terms on the license page control.
